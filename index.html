I build systems that matter — with people, planet, and purpose at heart.

I am a simple human on this wondrous Earth. I am the founder of StuffShare and an MSc International Human Resource Management student at Strathclyde Business School, with a BSc in Environmental Geoscience from the University of Edinburgh. What I care about is the climate crisis and the shift to a sustainable future. How I work on it is systems thinking, anchored in operations and management: looking at the bigger picture, asking what is missing from it, finding the leverage points, and building the processes and tools that remove that friction. I want to help build a reality where people are part of this incredible planetary ecosystem, living fulfilling lives together with other species in our one big shared home.
