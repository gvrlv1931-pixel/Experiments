About Me

I'm Nastya, a systems entrepreneur building circular economy infrastructure. I approach it with the people instincts of an HR professional, the rigour and curiosity of a scientist, and the care of someone who believes in the power of communities.

I'm the founder of StuffShare and an MSc International HRM student at Strathclyde Business School, with a BSc in Environmental Geoscience from Edinburgh. The climate crisis is what drives me. How I work on it is systems thinking anchored in operations and management: looking at the bigger picture, asking what's missing from it, finding the leverage points, and building the processes and tools that remove the friction. What I'm working toward is a world where people are genuinely part of the planetary ecosystem, living well alongside other species in our one shared home.

Outside of that, I'm chasing the feeling of being fully alive, usually up a mountain, through a new culture, or deep in a conversation that changes how I see something. I backpack, hitchhike, host strangers on my couch, and say yes to things I don't yet know how to do. My current one is improv.
